# Executive summary
This is the first cosmology paper from DESI, asking what the Year-1 baryon acoustic oscillation (BAO) measurements say about the expansion history and whether dark energy behaves like a cosmological constant. The data are BAO distance ratios in seven redshift bins between z = 0.1 and 4.2, from over six million galaxy, quasar and Lyman-alpha forest redshifts, measured under a blinded pipeline. These are fitted, alone and with Planck/ACT CMB data, a BBN baryon-density prior and three Type Ia supernova compilations, to flat and curved LCDM, flat wCDM, and flat and curved w0waCDM models, plus neutrino extensions. In flat LCDM the BAO alone give Omega_m = 0.295 +/- 0.015; with a BBN prior they give H0 = 68.53 +/- 0.80 km/s/Mpc, the most precise value not using CMB anisotropies, tightening to 68.52 +/- 0.62 with the CMB acoustic scale, both over 3 sigma from SH0ES. A constant w is consistent with -1. The headline is that when w is allowed to evolve, DESI+CMB prefers w0 > -1 and wa < 0 at 2.6 sigma, a preference that persists or grows with supernovae: 2.5, 3.5 and 3.9 sigma for Pantheon+, Union3 and DES-SN5YR. DESI+CMB also tightens the neutrino-mass bound to below 0.072 eV. The authors call this tantalizing but stress that unaccounted systematics or dataset inconsistencies must be ruled out, that the neutrino bound is prior- and model-dependent, and that the apparent phantom regime at z > 1, although also seen in parametrisation-independent analyses, could be reproduced by non-phantom quintessence or modified-gravity models.

## Claims
- claim: DESI Year-1 BAO alone are consistent with flat LCDM, giving a matter density Omega_m = 0.295 +/- 0.015 and r_d h = 101.8 +/- 1.3 Mpc.
  quote: "DESI BAO data alone are consistent with the standard flat ΛCDM cosmological model with a matter density"
  loc: "Abstract"
- claim: Allowing a time-varying equation of state, DESI combined with the CMB or with supernovae prefers w0 > -1 and wa < 0, at 2.6 sigma for DESI+CMB and 2.5, 3.5 and 3.9 sigma when Pantheon+, Union3 or DES-SN5YR are added.
  quote: "combinations of DESI with CMB or with type Ia supernovae (SN Ia) individually prefer w0 > −1 and wa < 0"
  loc: "Abstract"
- claim: With a BBN prior alone, DESI BAO give H0 = 68.53 +/- 0.80 km/s/Mpc, the most precise H0 that does not use CMB anisotropy information; adding the CMB acoustic scale theta_* tightens this to 68.52 +/- 0.62 km/s/Mpc.
  quote: "the most precise measurement to date that does not rely on information from CMB anisotropies"
  loc: "Sec. 8"
- claim: The BAO scales were measured under a blind analysis, with catalog-level blinding for discrete tracers and an additive shift of the correlation function for the Lyman-alpha sample, to mitigate confirmation bias.
  quote: "To mitigate confirmation bias, a blind analysis was implemented to measure the BAO scales."
  loc: "Abstract"
- claim: The single most anomalous data point, the LRG F_AP measurement at z = 0.51 which is offset by about 2 sigma from LCDM, is treated as a statistical fluctuation that does not drive the conclusions.
  quote: "This appears to be a simple statistical fluctuation and we do not regard it as significant."
  loc: "Sec. 3.2"
- claim: The dynamical dark energy signal is not attributed to a single dataset; all three of BAO, CMB and SN Ia contribute, and the three SN compilations give mutually consistent results.
  quote: "All three of the primary BAO, CMB and SN Ia probes contribute partially to this tension and the results including the 3 SN Ia datasets are mutually consistent with each other."
  loc: "Sec. 8"

## Methods
- BAO scale fitted in seven redshift bins from galaxy, quasar and Lyman-alpha correlation functions, reported as D_M/r_d, D_H/r_d (or D_V/r_d for BGS and QSO) and combined into a Gaussian likelihood.
- Blind analysis: redshifts and weights shifted at the catalog level for discrete tracers, additive shift of the Lyman-alpha correlation function; unblinding only after validation tests passed.
- MCMC parameter inference (cobaya/CAMB) over flat and curved LCDM, flat wCDM, flat and curved w0waCDM and neutrino extensions, with wide flat priors and w0 + wa < 0 imposed; parametrised post-Friedmann treatment for phantom crossing.
- Model comparison via Delta chi-square at the MAP, Bayes factors from PolyChord nested sampling and the Deviance Information Criterion.
- External combinations with a BBN prior on Omega_b h^2, the CMB acoustic scale theta_*, full Planck plus ACT lensing likelihoods and three SN Ia compilations.

## Data
- DESI Data Release 1 (first year of the main survey): over 6 million extragalactic redshifts, tracers BGS, LRG, ELG, QSO and Lyman-alpha forest, 0.1 < z < 4.2.
- Planck 2018 temperature and polarisation plus Planck PR4 and ACT DR6 CMB lensing.
- Type Ia supernova compilations Pantheon+, Union3 and DES-SN5YR, used one at a time.
- SDSS/BOSS/eBOSS BAO used for comparison and in a combined DESI+SDSS variant (Appendix A).

## Limitations
- limit: The authors state that systematic errors or inconsistencies among the datasets have not been excluded as the source of the dynamical dark energy preference.
  quote: "It remains important to thoroughly examine unaccounted-for sources of systematic uncertainties or inconsistencies between the different datasets that might be contributing to these results."
  loc: "Sec. 8"
- limit: The preference for phantom-like w(z) < -1 at z > 1 should not be over-interpreted, since some preferred (w0, wa) values may map onto non-phantom quintessence or modified-gravity models.
  quote: "However, we caution against over-interpretation of this result, since at least some of the (w0, wa) models preferred by our analysis could be mapped to quintessence models"
  loc: "Sec. 5.2"
- limit: DESI BAO on their own cannot separate w0 from wa; the w0waCDM result relies on combination with CMB and/or supernovae.
  quote: "DESI alone does not have sufficient power to break the degeneracy between w0 and wa"
  loc: "Sec. 5.2"
- limit: The neutrino-mass upper bound depends strongly on the chosen prior on the mass sum and relaxes in extended background models.
  quote: "We also caution that the upper bound obtained is strongly dependent on the choice of prior for P mν."
  loc: "Sec. 7.1"

## Open questions
- Whether the full range of (w0, wa) values preferred by the data can be mapped to physical models that avoid the phantom regime (Sec. 5.2).
- Whether unaccounted systematics or inconsistencies between BAO, CMB and SN Ia datasets are contributing to the departure from LCDM (Sec. 8).
- What the DESI full-shape clustering analyses will add, both for dark energy and for the neutrino mass through power-spectrum suppression (Sec. 7.1 and Sec. 8).
- Whether cosmology can discriminate the normal from the inverted neutrino hierarchy, since the current data only mildly favour the normal ordering (Sec. 7.1).
